About

Teaching Arabic as a living language, not a memorized code.

I'm Osama, an Arabic language instructor based in Cairo, working with non‑native speakers who want to actually use Modern Standard Arabic — reading, writing, and holding real conversations, not just passing a grammar quiz.

My own path runs through Al‑Azhar University, where I studied Sharia and Law and I'm now continuing a Pre‑Master's in Usul al‑Fiqh. That grounding in classical texts shapes how I teach: precise with grammar, but always tying it back to how Arabic is actually read, recited, and spoken.

I teach across both online and face‑to‑face classrooms, in private sessions and small groups, for kids and adults alike. Lessons are built around communicative methods and real assessment, with AI tools woven in for practice material, listening exercises, and faster feedback loops.
